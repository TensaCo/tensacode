01Gets better with use

It learns from your team.

When someone fixes an answer, that fix isn't lost in a chat log. It's saved as a lesson, and your AI trains on it. Results count too: when an action works out, or doesn't, it learns from that.

  • Corrections become training. Every fix is saved, along with who made it.
  • Learns from what actually happened. Real outcomes are feedback, not just opinions.
  • Improves on your schedule. Retrain when you choose, on your own machines.

02Shows its receipts

Every answer comes with receipts.

You shouldn't have to take an AI's word for it. TensorCode keeps what your documents actually say apart from what the AI thinks, and links every answer back to where it came from.

  • Sources on every answer. See the log, email, or report behind it.
  • Double-checks itself. Answers are checked against those sources before you see them.
  • Says “not sure” instead of bluffing. If the check fails, it tells you so.
  • Changes its mind when the facts change. Correct a source and the answer updates. The old version stays on record.

The checks are done by software, so they can still be wrong. That's exactly why the receipts are always there.

03Yours to keep

Your AI. Your servers. No lock-in.

The AI you train is saved as ordinary files that belong to you. Run it on your own hardware, even with no internet connection. Copy it, back it up, move it. You never have to rent it back.

  • Works offline. Your data stays in your building unless you choose to send it.
  • Owned, not rented. Save any version and bring it back exactly as it was.
  • Free and open source. MIT license: use it, change it, ship it.
  • Fits the tools your engineers already use. Python and TypeScript.
