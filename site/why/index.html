Why TensorCode

Most AI asks for your trust. Ours shows its work.

Most AI tools hand you an answer and ask you to take it on faith. They forget your team's corrections, and they live on someone else's servers. TensorCode was built to fix all three.

What it's built on

Three rules, no exceptions.

Show the work

An answer without sources is a guess. Every answer points back to the evidence behind it, so anyone can check it.

Learn from people

Your experts already know when something is wrong. The software should learn from them, not work around them.

Owned, not rented

If your team taught it, your business owns it. No lock-in, and no data held hostage.

Open by default

Anyone can check our claims.

TensorCode is free and open source under the MIT license. Anyone can read the code, run it, and test what we say about it.

We publish our measurements, including the ones that didn't go our way. If something doesn't work yet, we'd rather you read it from us.
