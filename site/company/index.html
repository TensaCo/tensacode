Company

We build AI a business can check.

TensaCo is the company behind TensaCode: open-source software for AI that shows its receipts, learns from the people who use it, and belongs to the business that trains it.

What we believe

Three rules we build by.

Show the work

An answer without sources is a guess. Every answer should point back to the evidence behind it.

Learn from people

The experts on your team already know when something is wrong. Software should learn from them, not work around them.

Owned, not rented

If your team taught it, your business should own it. No lock-in, and no data held hostage.

Open by default

Anyone can check our claims.

TensaCode is free and open source under the MIT license. Anyone can read the code, run it, and test what we say about it.

We publish our measurements, including the ones that didn't go our way. If something doesn't work yet, we'd rather you read it from us.
